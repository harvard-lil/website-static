We are hiring a project manager to help our team bring DOI to the legal community. Perma.cc has preserved the legal field's citations to the web for over a decade. Now, we are working to add the ability to mint DOIs for published scholarship as part of the Perma suite of offerings. This role will join the effort and guide the implementation, roll out, and communications for the project. 

To learn more about this role and apply, visit [Harvard's Career Portal.](https://careers.harvard.edu/job/perma-cc-legal-digital-scholarship-project-manager-in-cambridge-ma-united-states-jid-1551)

In addition to Harvard’s standard application form, please provide a short cover letter explaining how your career trajectory and interests align with this work and mission.
