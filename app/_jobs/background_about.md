The Library Innovation Lab is a team of librarians, programmers, artists, and lawyers with a broad mission: to build open-source tools and services to advance open knowledge.

We currently support three long-running services ([Perma](https://perma.cc), [H2O Open Casebook](https://opencasebook.org), and the [Caselaw Access Project](https://case.law)) as well as a range of shorter-term experiments.
